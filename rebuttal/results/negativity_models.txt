  R2.8 - REACTION NEGATIVITY AS THE OUTCOME
==============================================================================

C  = Consensus Index (published outcome)
D1 = reaction negativity of the target post
D2 = D1 plus the leave-one-out content score
D3 = reaction negativity, beta-binomial on raw counts

NOTE: CI is high when reactions are homogeneous; negativity is high when they
are hostile. Because CI = |1 - 2*f_neg|, a predictor that lowers consensus by
raising negativity flips sign between the two columns. Signs are EXPECTED to
be opposite.

If the leave-one-out score were adding information beyond content identity,
the content intercept SD would not change much when it is added. Compare
D1 (no score) with D2 (score added).

-- variance components at the zero boundary (|sd| < 0.01 ) --
 model        group sd_intercept sd_toxicity
    D3 account_name       0.4135    0.002339

These fits converged with a positive-definite Hessian AND carry a variance
component estimated at zero; the two are not in conflict. The fixed effects
remain interpretable, but the random-effect structure is over-specified for
this outcome and the corresponding random slope should not be interpreted.
